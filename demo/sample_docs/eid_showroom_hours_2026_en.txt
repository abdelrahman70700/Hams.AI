Saraya Living - Showroom hours during Eid al-Adha 2026

All Saraya Living showrooms close on the first day of Eid al-Adha.
From the second to the fourth day of Eid, showrooms open from 4 PM to 11 PM.
Deliveries and installations booked during Eid are moved to the first working day after the holiday, at no extra charge.
Online orders can still be placed during Eid; the customer service line 920004488 answers from 4 PM to 10 PM.
