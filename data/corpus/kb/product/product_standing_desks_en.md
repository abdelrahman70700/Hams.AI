# ErgoPro Height-Adjustable Desks

| Model | Code | Motors | Height range | Max load | Top sizes | Price (incl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ErgoPro Rise | ED-120 | Single motor | 72–118 cm | 70 kg | 120×60, 140×70 cm | SAR 1,850 |
| ErgoPro Rise Duo | ED-220 | Dual motor | 62–127 cm | 120 kg | 140×70, 160×80 cm | SAR 2,990 |
| ErgoPro Bench | ED-400 | Dual motor per seat | 65–125 cm | 100 kg per seat | 2- or 4-person bench | SAR 5,400 per 2 seats |

## Features

- Memory keypad with 4 height presets (ED-220 and ED-400).
- Anti-collision sensor that stops and reverses the desk when it meets an obstacle.
- Noise below 50 dB while moving.

## Safety notes

Do not exceed the maximum load. Keep cables loose enough to allow full height travel. Each motor should not run for more than 2 minutes in every 18 minutes (duty cycle 10%); the controller enforces a cool-down and shows "HOT" on the keypad when it is reached.

## Warranty

Frame: 5 years. Motors and controller: 3 years. Desktop surface: 2 years.
