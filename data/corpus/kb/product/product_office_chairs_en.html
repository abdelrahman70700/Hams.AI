Adjusting your chair

  1. Set seat height so your feet are flat on the floor and knees are at about 90 degrees.
  2. Adjust seat depth to leave two to three fingers of space behind the knees (EP-300 and EP-700 only).
  3. Position lumbar support at the curve of your lower back.
  4. Set armrests so your shoulders are relaxed when typing.
