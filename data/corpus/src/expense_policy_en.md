# Employee Travel and Expense Policy

Internal policy for Saraya Living employees. Owner: Finance Department. Version 3.2, effective 1 March 2026.

## Approval

All business travel must be approved in the HR system at least 5 working days before departure by the employee's line manager. International travel also requires approval from the department director.

## Hotel limits per night (including taxes)

| City | Staff | Managers and above |
|---|---|---|
| Riyadh | SAR 650 | SAR 900 |
| Jeddah | SAR 600 | SAR 850 |
| Dammam / Al Khobar | SAR 550 | SAR 750 |
| Other Saudi cities | SAR 450 | SAR 600 |
| GCC capitals | SAR 800 | SAR 1,100 |

## Daily allowance (per diem)

Employees receive a per diem of SAR 250 per day for domestic travel and SAR 400 per day for international travel, covering meals and local transport. Meals that are provided by a client or at a conference are deducted at SAR 60 per meal.

## Flights

Economy class for flights under 4 hours. Managers and above may book business class for flights longer than 4 hours.

## Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of returning. Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary if approved before the 20th of the month.
