# Procurement and Purchase Approval Policy

Internal policy. Owner: Procurement Department. Effective 1 April 2026.

## Approval thresholds

| Purchase value (excl. VAT) | Quotations required | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Up to SAR 10,000 | 1 | Department manager |
| SAR 10,001 – 100,000 | 3 written quotations | Department director and Procurement manager |
| SAR 100,001 – 1,000,000 | Formal tender (RFP) | Chief Financial Officer |
| Above SAR 1,000,000 | Formal tender (RFP) | CEO and Board procurement committee |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay under a threshold is prohibited and is treated as a policy violation.

## Supplier registration

New suppliers must provide a valid commercial registration, a VAT certificate, and bank details on company letterhead. Registration takes up to 7 business days. Saraya gives preference to local Saudi suppliers when price and quality are comparable, in line with local content targets.

## Payment terms

Standard supplier payment terms are 60 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Early payment within 15 days may be agreed in exchange for a discount of at least 2%.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare any relationship with a supplier before participating in an evaluation. Gifts from suppliers above SAR 200 in value must be declined or reported to Compliance.
