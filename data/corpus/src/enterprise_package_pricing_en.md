# Saraya Business Plans and Pricing 2026

Saraya Business provides furnished workspaces as a subscription: furniture, installation, maintenance, and replacements for a fixed monthly fee. Prices are in Saudi riyals (SAR), exclude 15% VAT, and are valid for contracts signed between 1 January and 31 December 2026.

## Plans

| Plan | Workstations | Monthly fee | Support tier | Minimum term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | up to 20 | SAR 2,900 | Standard | 12 months |
| Professional | 21 to 75 | SAR 7,500 | Premium | 12 months |
| Enterprise package | 76 and above | from SAR 18,000 (custom quote) | Enterprise | 24 months |

## What every plan includes

- Ergonomic chairs and desks from the ErgoPro range
- Free delivery and installation in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam
- Preventive maintenance visits (Starter: twice a year; Professional: quarterly; Enterprise: monthly)
- Replacement of defective items within the SLA

## Enterprise package details

The Enterprise package is priced per site. The base fee of SAR 18,000 per month covers up to 100 workstations; each additional workstation costs SAR 140 per month. It includes a dedicated Technical Account Manager, a quarterly business review, custom finishes in company colours, and one free full-floor layout change per year. Multi-city deployments receive a 7% discount on the total monthly fee for three or more sites.

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price |
|---|---|
| Premium support for Starter | SAR 1,200 per month |
| Meeting room package (table, 8 chairs, screen mount) | SAR 650 per room per month |
| Acoustic phone booth | SAR 480 per booth per month |
| Weekend installation | SAR 1,500 per visit |

## Payment terms

Invoices are issued monthly in advance and are payable within 30 days. Annual prepayment receives a 5% discount.
