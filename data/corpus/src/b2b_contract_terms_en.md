# Saraya Business Contract Terms (Summary)

This summary describes the main commercial terms of the Saraya Business subscription agreement. The signed contract prevails in case of any difference.

## Term and renewal

Contracts start on the installation completion date. Starter and Professional plans have a minimum term of 12 months; the Enterprise package has a minimum term of 24 months. Contracts renew automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

## Early termination

A customer who terminates before the end of the minimum term pays an early termination fee equal to three months of the current monthly fee, plus the cost of any custom-finished items, which are charged at 40% of their list price.

## Scaling up and down

Customers can add workstations at any time; new items are installed within 10 business days in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam. Reductions of up to 20% of workstations are allowed once per contract year without penalty, with 30 days' notice.

## Price changes

Prices are fixed during the minimum term. On renewal, Saraya may adjust prices by no more than 6% per year, with notice given at least 90 days before the renewal date.

## Ownership and damage

All furniture remains the property of Saraya. Normal wear and tear is covered. Damage caused by misuse is charged at repair cost, or at the depreciated value if the item must be replaced.

## Governing law

The agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and disputes are referred to the competent courts in Riyadh.
