# Company Holiday Calendar 2026

Applies to head office and support departments. Showroom and warehouse staff follow the operational rota and receive compensatory days off instead.

| Holiday | Dates (2026) | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Founding Day | Sunday 22 February | 1 |
| Eid al-Fitr | Thursday 19 March to Wednesday 25 March (subject to moon sighting) | 5 working days |
| Eid al-Adha | Monday 25 May to Sunday 31 May (subject to moon sighting) | 5 working days |
| Saudi National Day | Wednesday 23 September | 1 |

If an official holiday falls on a Friday or Saturday, a compensatory day is granted on the nearest working day, as announced by HR.

Showrooms close on the first day of each Eid and reopen on the second day from 4 PM. Customer service by phone is closed on the first day of each Eid; app chat remains available.

Exact Eid dates are confirmed by HR circular once the official announcement is made.
