# Shipping to GCC Countries

Saraya Living ships furniture from its Dammam distribution centre to selected Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries.

## Destinations, fees and delivery times

| Country | Cities served | Shipping fee | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bahrain | Manama and all governorates | SAR 450 per order | 5–8 business days |
| Kuwait | Kuwait City, Hawalli, Ahmadi | SAR 650 per order | 7–12 business days |
| United Arab Emirates | Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah | SAR 750 per order | 8–14 business days |
| Qatar | Doha | SAR 700 per order | 8–14 business days |

Oman is not served at the moment.

## Customs and duties

Prices on the website include Saudi VAT. For GCC shipments, Saudi VAT is removed at checkout and the destination country's VAT and any customs fees are paid by the customer to the courier on delivery.

## Installation and returns

Installation is not included for GCC orders. Returns from GCC countries are accepted only for defective items; change-of-mind returns are not possible outside Saudi Arabia.
