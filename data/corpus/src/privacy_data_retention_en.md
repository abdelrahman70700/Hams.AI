# Customer Data Privacy and Retention Policy

Saraya Living processes personal data in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

## What we collect

Name, mobile number, email, delivery addresses, order history, and — for installation visits — photos of the installation area when the customer sends them. We do not store full payment card numbers.

## Retention periods

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Order and invoice records | 10 years (tax and accounting requirements) |
| Customer service call recordings | 12 months |
| Installation photos | 6 months after the warranty claim or installation is closed |
| Marketing preferences | Until the customer unsubscribes, plus 30 days |
| Inactive customer accounts | Deleted after 3 years without any purchase or login |

## Customer rights

Customers can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their data by emailing privacy@saraya-living.sa. Requests are answered within 30 days. Deletion does not apply to records we are legally required to keep, such as invoices.

## Sharing with third parties

Data is shared only with delivery partners, the payment provider and the financing partner, and only the minimum needed to fulfil the order. Saraya does not sell customer data.
