# Saraya Office Relocation Service

Saraya Business offers a turnkey office move: dismantling, packing, transport, and reinstallation of furniture in the new location.

## Pricing

| Office size | Price (excl. VAT) | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 20 workstations | SAR 6,500 | 1 weekend |
| 21–60 workstations | SAR 14,000 | 1–2 weekends |
| More than 60 workstations | Custom quote after survey | Planned with the customer |

Moves between cities (for example Riyadh to Jeddah) are charged an additional SAR 3,500 per truck. One truck carries about 15 workstations including chairs and pedestals.

## What is included

Labelling plan, packing materials, insurance for transported items up to SAR 500,000 per move, and disposal of packaging. IT equipment (computers and screens) is moved, but disconnection and reconnection of IT equipment is the customer's responsibility.

## Discounts

Active Saraya Business subscribers on the Professional plan receive a 15% discount, and Enterprise package customers receive one free relocation of up to 60 workstations during the contract term.

## Booking

Book at least 3 weeks before the move date through the Saraya Business Portal or your account manager.
