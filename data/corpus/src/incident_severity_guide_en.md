# Incident Severity Guide (P1–P4)

Every Saraya Business support ticket is assigned a priority level when it is created. The priority determines which SLA target applies.

## Priority definitions

- **P1 – Critical:** A safety hazard or a complete inability to use a workspace. Examples: a collapsed shelving unit, a broken height-adjustable desk motor stuck at an unsafe angle, or a meeting room that cannot be used for a scheduled board meeting.
- **P2 – High:** A major function is impaired for several users but a workaround exists. Example: 10 or more chairs with failed gas lifts on one floor.
- **P3 – Medium:** A single item is defective or a minor function is impaired. Example: one drawer pedestal with a broken lock.
- **P4 – Low:** Cosmetic issues, questions, and general requests. Example: a scratched desk surface or a request for a care guide.

## Who sets the priority

The customer proposes a priority when opening the ticket. The Saraya service desk may adjust it within 30 minutes after review. If the customer disagrees, they can escalate to their account manager, and for Enterprise customers to the Technical Account Manager.

## Escalation path

1. Service desk agent
2. Shift supervisor (after the first response target is missed)
3. Account manager (after 50% of the resolution target has elapsed)
4. Head of Customer Operations (P1 tickets not resolved within the target)
