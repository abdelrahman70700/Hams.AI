# Information Security Policy for Employees

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the CRM, the HR system and VPN access. Passwords no longer expire on a fixed schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access company email only through the approved mobile app.

## Data classification

| Class | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Product catalogue, published prices | No restrictions |
| Internal | Policies, internal guides | Share only with employees |
| Confidential | Customer data, contracts, salaries | Encrypt when sending outside the company |
| Restricted | Payment data, security keys | Access only for named roles; never by email |

## Reporting incidents

Report suspected phishing or lost devices to the IT service desk within 1 hour of discovery, by phone at extension 4400 or through the ServiceDesk portal. Lost devices are remotely wiped after confirmation.
