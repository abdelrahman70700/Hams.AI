# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Saraya Living. This guide covers your first 90 days.

## Day 1

- Collect your badge and laptop from the IT desk on the 2nd floor of the head office (Olaya, Riyadh) at 8:30 AM.
- Activate MFA on your email and the HR system before noon; access is blocked after 24 hours without MFA.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, who is assigned by your line manager.

## First week

Complete the mandatory e-learning modules in the HR system: Information Security (45 minutes), Code of Conduct (30 minutes), and Customer Data Privacy (30 minutes). Showroom staff also complete Product Knowledge Level 1 within the first 10 days.

## Probation

The probation period is 90 days. Your manager holds check-ins at day 30 and day 60 and a formal review at day 85. Annual leave can be requested only after probation, except in emergencies approved by HR.

## Benefits enrolment

Medical insurance (class B for staff, class A for managers) starts on day 1. Add dependants within 30 days of joining; later additions are possible only at the annual renewal in January. Employees also receive a 25% staff discount on Saraya products, up to SAR 20,000 of purchases per year.
