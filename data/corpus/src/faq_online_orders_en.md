# Online Orders — Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I modify my order after placing it?

You can change items, colours or the delivery address within 2 hours of placing the order from the "My Orders" page. After 2 hours, contact customer service; changes are possible only if the order has not yet been dispatched from the warehouse.

## How do I track my order?

You receive a tracking number by SMS when the order leaves the warehouse. Track it in the app under "Track Orders" or on the website. The driver calls you about 60 minutes before arrival.

## Why was my order split into several deliveries?

Items stocked in different warehouses, or made-to-order items, may arrive separately. You only pay the delivery fee once per order.

## Do you sell gift cards online?

Yes. Digital gift cards from SAR 100 to SAR 5,000 are available. They are valid for 12 months and cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash.

## Is my payment information stored?

Card details are processed by our PCI-DSS certified payment provider. Saraya does not store full card numbers.

## Who do I contact for a problem with an online order?

Use the live chat in the app (24/7) or call 920004488 between 9 AM and 10 PM.
