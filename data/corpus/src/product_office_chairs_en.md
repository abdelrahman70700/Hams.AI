# ErgoPro Office Chairs — Product Sheet

## Models

| Model | Code | Max user weight | Retail price (incl. VAT) | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ErgoPro Lite | EP-100 | 110 kg | SAR 690 | Mesh back, fixed armrests, 2-year warranty on gas lift |
| ErgoPro Plus | EP-300 | 130 kg | SAR 1,290 | Adjustable lumbar support, 3D armrests, seat depth adjustment |
| ErgoPro Executive | EP-700 | 150 kg | SAR 2,450 | Leather upholstery, headrest, synchro-tilt with 5 lock positions |

All models are certified to the BIFMA X5.1 standard and come in black and grey. The Executive model is also available in tan leather for an extra SAR 200.

## Adjusting your chair

1. Set seat height so your feet are flat on the floor and knees are at about 90 degrees.
2. Adjust seat depth to leave two to three fingers of space behind the knees (EP-300 and EP-700 only).
3. Position lumbar support at the curve of your lower back.
4. Set armrests so your shoulders are relaxed when typing.
