# Saraya Business Support Tiers and Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This document defines the support tiers available to Saraya Business customers (office furnishing and workspace services) and the service levels Saraya commits to. Effective from 1 February 2026.

## Support tiers

| Tier | Coverage hours | Channels | Included in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Sunday–Thursday, 8:00–17:00 (Riyadh time) | Portal, email | Starter plan |
| Premium | 24/7 for P1, business hours for P2–P4 | Portal, email, phone hotline | Professional plan, or add-on for SAR 1,200/month |
| Enterprise | 24/7 for all priorities | Portal, email, hotline, dedicated Technical Account Manager | Enterprise package |

## Response and resolution targets

| Tier | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 8 business hours | 5 business days |
| Premium | 1 hour | 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | 15 minutes for P1, 1 hour for P2–P4 | 4 hours for P1, 8 business hours for P2–P4 |

Business hours are Sunday to Thursday, 8:00–17:00 Riyadh time, excluding official Saudi public holidays. Priority definitions (P1–P4) are described in the Incident Severity Guide.

## Service credits

If Saraya misses the resolution target for a Premium or Enterprise ticket, the customer receives a service credit of 5% of that month's subscription fee per missed ticket, capped at 25% of the monthly fee. Credits must be claimed within 30 days through the customer portal and are applied to the next invoice. Standard tier tickets are not eligible for service credits.

## Exclusions

SLA targets do not apply to tickets caused by customer-side modifications, to requests for new furniture or layout changes (handled as change requests), or during scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.
