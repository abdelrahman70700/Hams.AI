# Sustainability Highlights 2025

## Materials

In 2025, 62% of the wood used in Saraya products came from FSC-certified sources, up from 48% in 2024. The target for 2027 is 85%.

## Take-back and reuse

The furniture take-back programme collected 4,300 items in 2025. Of these, 71% were refurbished and donated or resold through the Clearance section, and the rest were recycled. Returned mattresses from the 100-night trial programme are sanitised and donated to charities.

## Energy and logistics

Solar panels installed on the Riyadh warehouse in June 2025 now supply about 30% of its electricity. The delivery fleet added 12 electric vans in Riyadh; electric vans completed 18% of Riyadh deliveries in the fourth quarter.

## Packaging

Plastic packaging per delivery was reduced by 35% compared with 2023 by switching to reusable furniture blankets for local deliveries.

## People

Saraya employed 1,240 people at the end of 2025, of whom 41% were women in head-office roles, and Saudi nationals made up 58% of the total workforce.
