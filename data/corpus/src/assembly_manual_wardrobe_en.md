# Lamar 3-Door Wardrobe — Assembly Manual (Model LW-3D)

Free professional assembly is included with every delivery. This manual is provided for customers who relocate the wardrobe or prefer to assemble it themselves. Self-assembly does not void the warranty, but damage caused during self-assembly is not covered.

## Before you start

- Assembly needs two adults and takes about 2.5 hours.
- Tools: Phillips screwdriver, rubber mallet, and the Allen key supplied (4 mm).
- Dimensions when assembled: width 150 cm, height 220 cm, depth 60 cm. Weight: 118 kg.
- Check that the box contains bags A to F; bag F contains the anti-tip wall bracket.

## Steps

1. Lay the left and right side panels flat and attach the bottom panel using 6 cam locks from bag A.
2. Insert the two middle dividers and fix them with 8 dowels from bag B.
3. Attach the top panel, then slide the back panel into the grooves.
4. Stand the wardrobe upright with two people. Never lift it by the top panel.
5. Fix the anti-tip bracket (bag F) to the wall. This step is mandatory for safety.
6. Mount the hinges (bag C) and hang the three doors; adjust the hinge screws until the gaps are even (2–3 mm).
7. Install shelves, the hanging rail (bag D) and the handles (bag E).

## Troubleshooting

If the doors do not close evenly, the floor is probably not level: use the adjustable feet at the base to level the wardrobe before adjusting hinges.
