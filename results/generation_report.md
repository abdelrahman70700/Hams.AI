15 three-turn conversations), produced by `scripts/benchmark_generation.py`; tables copied from
`results/summary.md` (generated by `scripts/make_report.py` from `generation_before.json` and

`generation_items_after.jsonl` / `generation_items_before.jsonl`, a categorised error analysis in
`error_analysis.md` and the 30-question human evaluation in `human_eval_before.csv` / `human_eval_after.csv`.
